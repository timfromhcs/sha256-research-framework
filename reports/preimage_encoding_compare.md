# Reduced-Round SHA-256 Preimage Scaling

Generated by `tools/measure_scaling.py`. Every value below was measured on
the machine described under *Environment* by an actual run. Nothing is
extrapolated.

## Scope and honesty statement

> Full 64-round SHA-256 preimage resistance is 2^256 compression-function
> evaluations, approximately 1.16e77. That is not reachable by any
> engineering means; it is a physical bound. This campaign measures where
> a *direct* SAT encoding of the compression function stops closing, which
> is a different and much weaker question.
>
> No full SHA-256 preimage and no full SHA-256 collision is claimed here.
> Every positive result below was re-checked by the segregated
> `sha-verifier` engine before being recorded as verified.
